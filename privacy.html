Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This page describes the intended privacy practices of 414Unlock. A production deployment must complete and publish the final controller, processor, retention, and contact details before collecting personal documents.

Information we may process

Account details such as your name and email, authentication records, verification status, professional credentials or identity documents you choose to submit, device information required for a supported service, and service/payment status may be processed.

Purpose and access

Information should be used only for account security, identity or professional review, confirming appropriate authorization, preventing abuse, delivering supported services, and meeting legal obligations. Identity documents must be held in private storage and available only to authorized reviewers and services that need them.

Public website and repository

The public website source repository must never contain uploaded identity documents, passwords, access tokens, API secrets, or private verification records. A hidden page URL is not a security control; all data access and review decisions must be enforced by the backend.

Storage and retention

Production deployment must configure a retention period, deletion procedure, encryption and access controls for documents. Documents should not be placed in public URLs or application logs and should be deleted when no longer needed, subject to required records retention.

Automation and human review

Automated document processing, including OCR or AI-assisted name matching, should be used only as decision support. Material mismatches and uncertain results should be routed to authorized human review, with an appeal or correction process.

Your choices

Before production, provide a working contact method for access, correction, deletion requests and privacy questions. Avoid uploading unnecessary information and do not share documents belonging to another person without a lawful basis and permission.
