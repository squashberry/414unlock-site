Important — no-signal service

For new A12+ iPhone orders, the advertised option is currently No Signal Bypass. Wi-Fi, apps, internet browsing and internet-based calling may work, but ordinary mobile-network outgoing calls and SMS are not available. Previously verified devices may have different eligibility; this must be confirmed with the provider. 414Unlock currently lists all iPhone services as Wi-Fi-only / no-signal.
