From wallet to service status.

A practical, trackable process from the first top-up to the service update.

  1. Create your account

    Sign up and complete account verification before using paid services.

  2. Top up your wallet

    Submit your SquashberryPay transaction reference. Credit is added after manual payment confirmation.

  3. Submit device details

    Choose a listed service, enter your iPhone information and confirm you are authorised to request it.

  4. Track progress

    The service team reviews the request and updates its status in your account.
